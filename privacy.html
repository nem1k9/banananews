BANAN NEWS · Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

What data the app uses

The app connects only to the TikTok account of the BANAN NEWS channel owner. With the owner's permission it uses the TikTok scopes user.info.basic and video.publish to publish photo posts to that account.

What is stored

Only the OAuth refresh token and the account's open ID are stored, encrypted (AES-256), to publish scheduled posts. No passwords, contacts, messages, videos, followers or any other personal data are collected or stored.

Other people's data

The app does not collect, process or share data of any other TikTok users or website visitors. These pages use no cookies, analytics or trackers.

Sharing

Data is not sold or shared with third parties. It is used only to send posts to TikTok.

Deletion

The owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok settings (Security → Manage app permissions); the stored token then becomes invalid and is deleted.
