1. What the app is

BANAN NEWS is a private automation tool operated by the owner of the Telegram channel @BananNewws. Once a day it creates a photo carousel with Counter-Strike 2 news images made by the channel and publishes it to the channel owner's own TikTok account through the TikTok Content Posting API.

2. Who can use it

The app is used only by the channel owner to post to their own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public and does not post on behalf of other TikTok users.
